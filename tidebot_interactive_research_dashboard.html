The Problem

Floating plastic waste can accumulate in waterways and travel toward larger bodies of water. The project explores a small prototype that may assist in collecting floating debris in a controlled test environment.

Research Objectives

01

Design and develop a floating prototype for collecting floating plastic waste.

02

Integrate ultrasonic sensors to assist with obstacle-distance detection.

03

Evaluate the prototype’s collection performance through standardized testing.

04

Identify limitations and improvements for future versions.

How It Works

Proposed system sequence

1

Float
The platform is supported by its flotation elements.

2

Move
The propulsion system moves the prototype through the test area.

3

Detect
Two ultrasonic sensors measure distance to nearby obstacles.

4

Collect
The collection net is used to gather floating test material.

5

Measure
Collected material and trial observations are recorded for analysis.
